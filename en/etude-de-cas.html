High-Performance Rebuild for Northline

How we eliminated technical debt, cut load time by 4 and increased the conversion rate by 140% in 90 days.

+140% Overall conversion rate
0.4 s LCP load time
100/100 Google PageSpeed score

1. The challenge

Northline was running an aging website built on a heavyweight CMS with numerous redundant plugins. The result: load times over 4.2 seconds on mobile, a high bounce rate (68%) and poor visibility in search engines.

The goal was clear: rebuild the user experience from scratch around a strategy focused on raw speed, message clarity and optimization for AI-driven queries.

2. The Nexora technical solution

We went with a radical approach: zero heavy client-side frameworks, semantic HTML5, modern CSS and minimal JavaScript executed asynchronously.

  • Native Web Architecture: A structure stripped of all unnecessary code, reducing page weight from 3.2 MB to under 180 KB.
  • Semantic Markup & Schema.org: Full integration of rich structured data to maximize exposure in Google snippets and AI-generated answers (GEO).
  • Serverless Infrastructure: Edge deployment with server response times (TTFB) under 50 ms.
“Nexora's work transformed our online presence. The site loads instantly and our quote requests more than doubled in two months.”
— Northline Marketing Department

3. Measured results

Three months after the new version went live, analytics confirm the impact of the technical rebuild on the business:

  • Conversion: A 140% increase in qualified contact requests.
  • SEO & GEO: First-page rankings on 12 strategic keywords and frequent citations by AI engines.
  • Resources: Server carbon footprint reduced by 75%.
