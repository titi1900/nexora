FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Understanding the impact of strategic consulting.

How long does a strategic scoping engagement last?

Depending on the project's scope, a full scoping engagement generally runs over 2 to 6 weeks, with regular collaborative workshops.

Do you also get involved in project execution?

Yes. We ensure full continuity, from the initial strategy through technical development to analytics monitoring.

What deliverables are provided at the end of the engagement?

You receive a complete scoping package: functional specifications, budgeted roadmap, architecture diagrams and evaluation KPIs.

Can you audit a project that is already underway or stalled?

Absolutely. We run flash audits to identify technical or methodological blockers and get the project back on track efficiently.
