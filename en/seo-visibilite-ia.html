FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Understanding SEO and AI visibility.

What is optimization for AI engines (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) means structuring and writing content so that it is picked up as a trusted source by AI assistants when they answer users.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

The first effects on indexing and rankings generally appear within 2 to 4 months, depending on the sector's competitiveness and the site's initial authority.

Does SEO replace paid advertising (SEA)?

SEO builds lasting value and sustainable organic traffic without cost per click, while SEA provides an immediate lever. The two approaches are complementary.
