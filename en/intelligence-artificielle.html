RAG Architecture (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

We connect language models to your company's living memory (ERP, DMS, databases) without ever exposing your sensitive data externally.

01. INGESTION Secure vectorization of your confidential documents (PDF, APIs, SQL).
02. RETRIEVAL High-precision semantic search across VectorDB (pgvector / Qdrant).
03. SYNTHESIS Generation of reliable answers with verifiable sources and citations by the AI agent.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything about integrating AI in business.

Will our data be used to train public models?

No, that is an absolute priority for us. We use enterprise APIs with guaranteed confidentiality, or we deploy open-source models on your own servers.

How long does it take to deploy an initial POC?

Typically, a functional Proof of Concept (POC) is ready in 2 to 4 weeks to validate technical feasibility and business ROI before any large-scale rollout.

How do you prevent language model hallucinations?

Thanks to RAG architectures: the AI answers exclusively from the reference documents provided and always cites the source of its information.

Do you support change management and adoption?

Yes, we train your operational teams directly in prompt engineering and in the best practices for using their new tools.
