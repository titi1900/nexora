FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does a Nexora partnership work?

Do I need a website built by Nexora already?

No. We can take over an existing project, audit the code and propose a realistic improvement plan, even if we did not build the initial version.

What is the support response time?

On a monthly retainer, critical requests are handled within 24 to 48 business hours. Planned improvements follow a pace agreed on together.

Do you work with our in-house teams?

Yes. We know how to collaborate with in-place developers, product owners or marketers, bringing front-end, performance and SEO/GEO expertise.

Can the retainer be stopped or adjusted easily?

Yes. Retainers are flexible, with no opaque long-term commitment. We adjust the volume to your actual needs.
