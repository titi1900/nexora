FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clarity on how your interfaces are built.

Do you work with frameworks (React, Vue, Next…)?

Only when the product's complexity genuinely justifies it. In most cases, semantic HTML + modern CSS + a little JS is enough and delivers far better performance.

How long does a brochure website take?

A well-scoped 5 to 10-page site can be delivered in 3 to 6 weeks, including audits and testing.

Do you handle hosting and deployment?

Yes. We set up fast Edge/serverless hosting, security headers (CSP, etc.) and all the necessary redirects.

Will the site be accessible (WCAG)?

Yes. Contrast, keyboard navigation, ARIA attributes and semantic structure are built in from the design phase, not bolted on afterwards.
