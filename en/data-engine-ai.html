DataEngine AI: Semantic Structuring & Data Valorization

Building a high-performance ingestion pipeline that turns raw data into knowledge graphs usable by LLMs and next-generation search engines.

12M+ Events processed per day
99.8% AI categorization accuracy
× 3.5 Visibility in GEO engines

1. The business challenge

With the rise of AI agents and generative search (GEO), unstructured data has become a major barrier to visibility and decision-making. The DataEngine AI project set out to clean, classify and expose an industrial group's critical data streams in real time.

The goal: convert heterogeneous databases into a semantically accurate knowledge graph.

2. Deployment & technical building blocks

Streaming ETL Ingestion

Continuous collection through lightweight pipelines that process data without server overload.

Semantic Enrichment

Automatic tagging via small NLP models for instant JSON-LD / Schema.org typing.

Sovereign Vector Store

Vector storage hosted in France to guarantee confidentiality and GDPR compliance.

High-Capacity RAG API

Secure endpoints allowing internal applications to query the AI in < 100 ms.

3. Results & lasting performance

Thanks to the DataEngine AI pipeline, the client company automated 85% of its internal reporting tasks and saw its products' presence multiply by 3.5 in answers generated by mainstream AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).
